## In product work

**Contrast is checked, not guessed.** On QuickPay I tested every text and background pair against WCAG AA and fixed the ones that failed:

The action colour, Orange 600, was chosen because white text on it passes AA at 4.6 : 1. The brighter Orange 500 is kept for the logo and illustrations.

**Colour is never the only signal.** In Nūring, green, amber and red always come with a word: Flagged, Watch, Clear. In QuickPay, a pending payment is labelled as pending, not just coloured, and every failure says in words whether money moved.

**Large text doesn't break amounts.** QuickPay never cuts off or abbreviates an amount. The balance steps down in size instead, so a long figure stays whole at any text size.

**Motion has a calm version, and gestures have another way in.** Each of the four [QuickPay motion specs](/work/quickpay/) says what happens with reduced motion turned on. The swipe on a payment row has a version for people who can't swipe: VoiceOver and TalkBack list Repeat and Split as row actions, and on a keyboard Enter opens them and Escape closes them.

## On this site

I built this site myself, so I hold it to the same standard.

- **Contrast:** every text colour is measured in both light and dark mode, and all pass AA (4.5 : 1 or more). In November 2026 the faintest grey failed, at 3.3 : 1 in dark mode and 2.4 : 1 in light, so I darkened it until it passed.
- **Automated check:** I run axe on every main page, in both light and dark mode. The first run found 3 issues: a scrolling strip that couldn't be reached by keyboard, and two tables with an unnamed column. All three are fixed, and the latest run finds none. A check like this catches only part of what can go wrong, so I also test by hand.
- **Keyboard:** everything works without a mouse. That includes the live QuickPay screen and its mode switch, the motion demos and the swipe row, and the full-size image viewer, which takes focus and closes with Escape. The sideways photo strip can be focused and scrolled with the arrow keys, and the sticky header comes back as soon as focus moves into it.
- **Screen readers:** product videos are marked as decorative, because the title and summary beside them say what each project is. Every design system image uses its caption as its description. Tables have column and row headers, and the copies of the greeting used for its glitch effect are hidden, so the word is read once.
- **Reduced motion:** with it turned on, the greeting stays on "Hello", videos show a still image, the header stops sliding, and the demos fade instead of moving.
- **Touch and small screens:** hover effects only run on devices with a mouse or trackpad, so they never get stuck on a phone. The layout fits phone width without scrolling sideways.

## What isn't done yet

- The changing greeting and the looping videos have no pause button. They stop when reduced motion is on, but everyone should be able to pause them.
- I haven't tested this site with someone who relies on a screen reader every day. That's the next step, because automated tools and my own checks can't replace it.
