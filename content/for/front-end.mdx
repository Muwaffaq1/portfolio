## What you're hiring for, and where to look

**Design and build, by one person.** I design in Figma and ship in code. This site, [treeti](/work/treeti/), [PharmaQuest](/work/pharmaquest/), [Sarki Energy](/work/sarki-energy/) and [Almutamir](/work/almutamir/) were all designed and built by me.

**React, TypeScript, HTML and CSS you can read.** The code for this site, treeti, Sarki Energy and Skyline is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/Muwaffaq1). At Nūring I redesigned the web app and coded its front end on the design system I built.

**Testing, CI and deploys.** treeti runs lint, type checks, unit tests and Playwright end-to-end tests in Chromium and WebKit on every push. PharmaQuest syncs progress to a Cloudflare D1 database through Pages Functions. The details are on the [engineering page](/engineering/).

**Responsive and accessible.** Every main page here passes axe in light and dark mode, text contrast is measured per token, and the site works by keyboard and on phones. What I check and what I fixed is on the [accessibility page](/accessibility/).

**Working with engineers.** At Nūring I hand my front end to the backend engineers who connect the APIs. On QuickPay I handed over a file built for engineers, with matching token names in design and code and [motion specs](/work/quickpay/) for iOS and Android.

**Performance by default.** This site is static HTML on Cloudflare's network, videos only play while on screen, and images are sized for the screen with full-size versions on demand.
