## What you're hiring for, and where to look

**Reviewing and rebuilding a money app.** [QuickPay](/work/quickpay/) started as 105 screens with no system behind them. I audited them, decided what to keep, fix and rebuild, and rebuilt about 91 screens on [one token system](/systems/quickpay/) with dark mode.

**Every state, not just the happy path.** Sending, sent, failed, declined, offline, locked, under review and no results, straight from the delivered file, in the [QuickPay case study](/work/quickpay/).

**Rules for showing money.** Seven rules, from "every failure says whether money moved" to "a hidden balance never shows zero".

**Voice and error copy.** A guide built from the real copy: say whether money moved first, name the cause, give a next step and a time. It's in the same case study, with a do and don't table and a template for every error.

**Motion and haptics.** Four QuickPay moments, specified for engineers: balance reveal, send success, freeze card and a swipe on a payment, each with timings, springs, iOS and Android haptics and a reduced-motion version.

**iOS and Android.** The send flow on both platforms, side by side: what stays QuickPay and what follows each platform, from sheets and back navigation to Face ID and BiometricPrompt.

**A live money product.** [treeti](/work/treeti/) records interest-free loans between friends and family, and it's live with real people and real money recorded.

**What shipped vs what was designed.** Every case study says which parts are live and which were delivered as design, so you know exactly what you're looking at.
