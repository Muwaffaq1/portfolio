You can switch a real QuickPay screen between light, dark and Arabic on the [home page](/#systems).

**Complex problems made simple.** [treeti](/work/treeti/) turns an awkward money conversation between friends into a clear written agreement. [Sarki Energy](/work/sarki-energy/) explains eight business lines to eight very different visitors. [QuickPay](/work/quickpay/) starts with a full audit and a keep, fix, rebuild plan.

**Complex web apps and dashboards.** Nūring's staff triage and athlete views are built from the [design system](/systems/nuring/). I redesigned and coded Nūring's web app; the full app is confidential and available on request.

**Explaining your reasoning.** Every case study has a decision table with the trade-off each choice cost, from [Almutamir's](/work/almutamir/) one search bar to [Skyline's](/work/skyline/) rules for which card to show.

**Prototypes and micro-interactions.** Try the [QuickPay motion specs](/work/quickpay/) and the badge and level-up flow in [PharmaQuest](/work/pharmaquest/).

**Learning from real use.** treeti testers changed 8 flows within 10 days of launch, and the [case study](/work/treeti/) shows the live numbers.

**Using AI well.** How I use Claude Code and Figma's MCP server, and where I don't trust them, is in [How I work](/#process).
