## Why it exists

I built PharmaQuest for my friend's sister, a pharmacist preparing for the PEBC Pharmacist Qualifying Exam. That means working through hundreds of multiple-choice questions, mostly from PDFs and books. It's slow, and it's hard to tell what you've actually learned. She needed practice that felt like a short daily habit, brought back the questions she got wrong, and explained every answer.

## What it does

- **257 questions:** 96 official PEBC sample questions with answers from the official key, 151 written from the patient cases in *Applied Therapeutics* (12th ed.), and 10 from the PEBC references list.
- **Every option explained,** with a short "Remember" line after each answer.
- **Spaced repetition** brings missed questions back until they stick.
- **A reason to come back:** daily runs, XP, levels, streaks, a mastery map, boss battles and badges.

When I asked how it was going, she said:

> "AlhamduLillah. It's surpassed my expectations sef. Tell Muwaffaq JazakAllahu bi khair."

## The game layer

Studying for a licensing exam is a long grind, so the app rewards showing up, not just getting answers right.

- **A daily goal of 15 questions.** Questions you're due to review come first.
- **Streaks with freezes.** Every 7 days in a row earns a freeze, holding up to 2, so one missed day doesn't wipe out weeks of work.
- **XP and levels.** 10 XP per correct answer plus combo bonuses, climbing nine titles from Pharmacy Student to Pharmacotherapy Legend.
- **12 badge series, 41 tiers.** For habits, skill, knowledge and milestones, from Hot Hand (5 right in a row) to PEBC Ready (every official sample question).
- **Revenge Round** brings back every question you missed.
- **Boss Battle** unlocks after 30 answers: 20 questions, 3 lives and 75 seconds each.

## How it's built

React and Vite, deployed on Cloudflare Pages. Progress saves in the browser after every answer and syncs to a small Cloudflare database, so it follows you across devices. Settings include a read-only progress report and a backup download.

## What shipped vs what was designed

Shipped: the full practice app, live at pharmaquest.pages.dev. Still to do: the explanations and derived questions were written with AI help and haven't been reviewed by a pharmacist yet, so an in-app "Flag issue" button collects problems for review.
