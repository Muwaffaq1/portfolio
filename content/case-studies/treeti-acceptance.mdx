## The number that bothered me

Three weeks after treeti launched, 23 agreements had been written and 7 were still waiting for the borrower to accept. Nearly a third of the loans people took the trouble to write down were stuck one step from being real.

For a lender, that's the worst place to get stuck. They have done the work: entered the amount, picked the dates, maybe added a witness. Then nothing happens, and treeti gives them no way to tell whether the borrower ignored it, never got the link, or tried and gave up.

## Walking it as a borrower

I went through the flow as a borrower would, on a phone, from a WhatsApp message.

1. **A message from a friend.** "I've written down our agreement on treeti. Please review it here," with a link. treeti doesn't send invitations itself, so this message is the only way in.
2. **A page that says almost nothing.** "You're invited to review an agreement." It shows no lender's name, no amount and no due date. The only action is **Sign in to read**.
3. **A separate sign-in.** Email, then a code from the inbox, then back to the app.
4. **Then the terms.** Only now does the borrower see who it's from and what it says, and can accept.

The comparison that stood out was with witnesses. They get a lighter path: verify the email on the invite page itself, "no password or profile setup." The borrower, who has the most at stake and the least reason to rush, gets the heavier one.

These are hypotheses, not findings. The funnel shows where people leave, not why. Three short calls with lenders whose agreements are stuck would test them faster than any change I could ship.
