## The brief

Axis Urban designs modular housing, civic infrastructure, smart micro-grids and regenerative materials. The site had to make a technical, long-term mission feel concrete, and lead serious buyers to one action: asking for a proposal.

## What I made

A paid project: a seven-page site in Framer covering home, process, portfolio, insights, support, contact and a 404 page. I designed and built it.

The home page tells the story in order:

1. **The promise:** "Engineering the Future of Human Living," over full-bleed architecture.
2. **The problem:** cities will house three billion more people by 2050.
3. **Four sectors,** each with its own image and one line.
4. **The team,** to put people behind the engineering.
5. **The numbers,** set large so they read at a glance.
6. **Featured work** and the company's global footprint.
7. **One clear call to action:** Get a proposal.
