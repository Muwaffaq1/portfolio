## The brief

Attik is a sculptural, made-to-order furniture studio in Abuja. Its owner had just launched a site with a 3D studio, a room planner and a shop, and hired me to give it a new front door without replacing any of it.

So the work was one new homepage: an addition, not a redesign. Every route out of it leads to her existing pages, and nothing in it criticises the work already there.

## What I made

- A new homepage that tells the studio's story before showing products
- A launch film and short reels cut for social
- A small design system so the new page sits naturally beside the old one

## What shipped vs what was designed

Delivered: the homepage, built and live, plus the launch film and social reels for the studio's channels.
