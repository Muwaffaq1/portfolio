## The problem

Almutamir helps Nigerian pilgrims compare and book Hajj and Umrah packages from licensed agencies. The site at almutamir.com worked, but it didn't feel like something you'd trust with one of the most important journeys of your life. It opened with a four-field form before showing a single package, showed packages one at a time behind tabs, and its date field didn't appear on iPhone at all.

## The system

Host Grotesk for type, an ivory canvas with deep ink and emerald, and gold used sparingly. Spacing doubles at every step (2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128) and corners follow the same rhythm (8, 16, 32). The pages built on it: home, services, about and contact; packages and a package page with a booking sidebar that stays in view; a checkout with a price summary that stays in view; sign-in and sign-up; and terms, privacy and refunds.

## What shipped vs what was designed

Built in Next.js and Tailwind, and live on a preview link with every page from the homepage to checkout. almutamir.com still runs the old site, so there are no results to report yet. I also made a before-and-after film for the client to present the change.
