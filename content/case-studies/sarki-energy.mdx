## The brief

Sarki Energy is an integrated energy company: fuel and gas supply, power and renewables, engineering and infrastructure. The brief, received on 21 October 2026, asked for a corporate site that explains eight business lines to very different visitors, from a homeowner buying cooking gas to a government agency planning a project.

## The design system

One source of truth with three outputs: Figma variables for design, CSS tokens for the website, and a JSON file for future native apps. Light and dark come from the same tokens, so a change made once reaches every surface. The type is Host Grotesk and the icons are Phosphor, both open licensed.

## What shipped vs what was designed

Shipped: 26 page types, from the business lines, products and projects to insights, careers, the station locator, and working enquiry and quote forms. Parked for later: customer accounts and online ordering, which wait on the client's systems.
