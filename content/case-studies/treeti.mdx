## Why I wanted this to exist

Lending money to someone close is a kind thing that can quietly turn awkward. Nobody wants to ask for the terms in writing, because it sounds like you don't trust them. So the details live in a chat or in memory, and they drift.

I wanted lending to feel like a favour, not a contract: terms we both agreed to, someone we trust as a witness, and a clear record of every repayment. Interest-free, because the point is helping someone, not profiting from them. I couldn't find anything like that, so I built treeti.

## The problem for everyone else

People lend to family and friends all the time and settle the terms in a chat. Months later nobody agrees on the amount, the date or what was promised. Lending apps don't help: they move money, charge interest or score people. Nothing simply keeps an honest record that both sides agreed to.

## Key decisions

<Table head={["Decision", "Why", "What it cost"]} rows={[["Record money, never move it", "Holding money turns a trust tool into a bank", "No payments built in. ## What happened

> "I looked into it, and it is absolutely amazing. Especially the private debt record, that is the most important for me."
> — a treeti user

Most other feedback arrived as voice notes from people who loved the idea. I shipped quickly and let real people shape it. Within ten days, testers had changed the product: eight heavy flows were simplified, agreements gained ten currencies, "My debts" arrived for private records with reminders, and witness names and interest-free safeguards were built before anything else.
